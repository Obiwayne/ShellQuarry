# ShellQuarry promo: storyboard

20.0 s, 1080 × 1920 (9:16 for TikTok, Reels, Shorts and X), 60 fps = 1200 frames.
Path: hook → meet it → three things it does → end card.
Brand: Claude-dark ink #141413, clay #d97757 accent, Herdr blue #89b4fa and mauve #cba6f7 from the icon;
Space Grotesk headlines, Inter body.
Audio: female voiceover (Kokoro, local), original jazz bed ducked under the voice, a click on each tap.

All app footage is the real app (docs/screenshots, 1402 × 912 window captures), shown in a window card and moved with
pans and zooms. The key element of every shot stays inside the centre 1080 × 1080.

| # | Scene | On screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | Three agent rows pop in with status dots (working, needs you, idle). Title: "Running a few coding agents?" | "Running a few coding agents at once?" |
| 2 | Meet | Icon tile pops, "ShellQuarry" and "Herdr, with buttons."; the app window rises in below | "Meet ShellQuarry. Herdr, with buttons." |
| 3 | New agent | Window zooms into the New agent dialog; a tap lands on Start agent | "Start Claude Code, Codex or twenty-two more." |
| 4 | Browser | Pan to the browser panel; the agent's cursor glides to a link; "Agent is clicking" pill | "Give them a browser they drive while you watch." |
| 5 | Needs you | Zoom to the sidebar: the "Needs you" badge pulses, then the alert | "And see which one needs you." |
| 6 | End card | Icon, "ShellQuarry", "Free and open source · for Herdr", github.com/Obiwayne/ShellQuarry | "ShellQuarry. Free and open source." |

Timings are set in src/config.ts after recording the voiceover (voice/speak.py writes the line lengths).
